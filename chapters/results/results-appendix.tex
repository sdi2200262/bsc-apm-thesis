Table~\ref{tab:proc-time-paired-by-model} gives the model-specific paired time differences at session and phase scope. At session scope, APM was longer in all six pairs under each model, with a wider duration gap under Opus. Its active and idle components were higher in at least five of the six pairs in each group. At phase scope, the implementation phase was longer under APM in all six pairs of each model, with close median differences. For implementation duration, the Sonnet group had the higher Hodges-Lehmann estimate and mean. Planning remained mixed in sign under both models.
